THE SCIENTISTS

Who is behind Science Samples?

Pioneering the Next Era of Boom Bap

Science Samples was founded by a collective of platinum-selling producers and sound designers who felt that modern sample packs lacked the grit and soul of classic samplers like the MPC60 and SP-1200.

We process every sound through vintage analog gear, tape machines, and tube compressors to give you unparalleled warmth and texture. Our mission is to provide you with the exact sonic foundation you need to produce hits.
